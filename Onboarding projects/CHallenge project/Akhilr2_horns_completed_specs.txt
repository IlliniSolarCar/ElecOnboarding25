# Horn Breakout Board
**Board Requirements**

## Overview and Description
- **Primary Function:** The board's primary function is to act as a switch to power two 24V electronic horns connected in series.
- **Circuit Function:** It receives a low-voltage digital signal (Horn Control) and uses it to switch a higher current 24V line to activate the horns.
- **Horn Load:** Designed to drive two electronic horns in series, drawing a total of 150mA at 24V.
- **Other board integration things:**
    - **Low Voltage Bus Board:** Supplies the primary +24V power.
    - **Other Board:** Sends the Horn Control digital signal.

## High-Level Requirements
- **Microcontroller:**
    - **No Microcontroller (No ICs)**: This board does not contain a microcontroller; its logic is purely analog/switch-based.
- **Features:**
    - **Horn On/Off:** Must provide a switched output based on a digital control signal.
    - **Horn Output:** Must supply **+24V at 150mA** to the two horns in series.
    - **Current Limiting/Protection:** Must include a fuse for overcurrent protection.
    - **Noise Reduction:** Must include components to mitigate noise on the control signal.
    - **Diode Protection:** Must include a diode for protection against back-EMF (flyback voltage) generated by the inductive horn load.

## Communication Protocols
- **Digital GPIO:** Used to switch the horn ON or OFF.
    - **Requirement:** The control signal must be conditioned (filtered) to prevent noise from accidentally triggering the MOSFET.

## Connectors
- **General Requirement:** Connectors must be suitable for **at least 1A** of current capacity (to allow for the 2A fuse and provide margin).
- **Power In:** 1x3 Pin Connector
    - **Pinout:** **GND / +24V / GND** (3-pin for keyed power to prevent reverse polarity).
- **Horn Control:** 1x2 Pin Connector
    - **Pinout:** **GND / HORN_CTL**
- **Horn Out (1):** 1x2 Pin Connector
    - **Pinout:** **HORN_IN / HORN_OUT** (Connects to Horn 1)
- **Horn Out (2):** 1x2 Pin Connector
    - **Pinout:** **HORN_IN / HORN_OUT** (Connects to Horn 2, completing the series circuit).

## ICs
- **No ICs** are used on this board. Switching is performed by a single MOSFET.

## Buttons/Switches
- **Debug Button (Optional):** A push-button may be added, wired to the MOSFET's gate, to manually drive the `HORN_CTL` signal to GND or VCC for testing purposes.

## Power System
- **Source:** **+24V DC** from the LV Bus Board.
- **Protection:** Requires a **2A fuse** placed between the +24V rail and the horn circuit.
- **Filtering/Bulk Capacitance:** Needs a combination of decoupling and bulk capacitors:
    - 3x 0.1µF
    - 2x 1µF
    - 2x 10µF
    - 1x 100µF (Polarized Radial)
    *(All capacitors connected between +24V and GND.)*

## Test Points
- **Required Signals:**
    - **+24V**
    - **GND**
    - **HORN_CTL** (Control signal input)
    - **HORN_IN** (Power to the horn series circuit)

## LED Indicators
- **Required:**
    - **+24V** (To indicate power is present on the board)
- **Optional:**
    - **HORN_CTL** (To indicate when the control signal is active/high)
